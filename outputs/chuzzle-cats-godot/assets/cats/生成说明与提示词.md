# 小猫素材与生成提示词

生成方式：内置 GPT Image 工具；六个独立生成任务，透明背景参数为 true。没有使用原版游戏图片。

六张 PNG 位于本目录。它们是脸部底图，特意不包含眼睛；运行游戏后由 board_view.gd 叠加动态眼睛。不要把底图误认成缺失素材。

动画：每只猫的眨眼间隔约 3.4–6.4 秒，闭合和睁开速度不同；瞳孔轻微游移；选中的猫在鼠标处于棋盘内时看向鼠标；消除时短暂眯眼。支持“眼睛：动态/静态”按钮。

花纹和编号保留。动画计时不消耗游戏 RNG，不改变棋盘和补球。

缩小显示使用线性过滤与 mipmaps；六张素材均保留真实 alpha。

## 每次生成使用的共同提示词

Use case: stylized-concept. Production asset for a cozy cat match puzzle game in Godot. ONE isolated front-facing CAT HEAD ONLY, no body or paws. Premium charming softly shaded hand-painted 2D game sprite, plush rounded cheeks, little pointed ears, clean silhouette, crisp large fur patterns that remain recognizable at 80 pixels. Perfectly frontal, upright symmetrical geometry, soft warm top-left light, subtle tufted fur, not photorealistic. Square transparent canvas, actual alpha background, no shadow outside silhouette. Head including ears fits within x=8% to92%, y=8% to92%. Pink nose centered around (50%,65%), tiny smiling mouth below, short delicate whiskers. IMPORTANT animation-ready face layer: NO EYES, NO EYEBROWS, NO eyelids, NO sockets, NO white eye marks; leave ordinary smooth fur across both eye locations (35%,49%) and (65%,49%), because animated eyes will be drawn by the engine. Fur must continue naturally across these areas. No text, number, badge, collar, accessories, border, UI, watermark. High-quality polished lovable kitten face, broad simple shapes, not scary.
